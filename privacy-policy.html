Privacy Policy

Block Garden: Zen Puzzle (package com.zenleafgames.blockgarden)
Developer: Zen Leaf Games · Effective date: 29 September 2026

Summary

You can play Block Garden as a guest or sign in with your Google Play Games account. As a guest, your game progress is stored only on your device. If you sign in, we use your Google Play Games account to keep your scores, leaderboard ranking and game progress tied to you across devices. In both cases we collect a limited amount of data to run the game, its leaderboards, analytics and ads, as described below. We do not sell your personal data and we do not collect your e-mail address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Data we collect

  • Account and sign-in (via Unity Gaming Services and, if you sign in, Google Play Games Services): a game-specific player ID that Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) creates for every player — anonymous if you play as a guest — and a session token stored on your device. If you sign in with Google Play Games, also your Play Games player ID and display name. Used to sign you in, keep your progress and show your profile.
  • Guest play: if you continue as a guest, no Google Play Games data is collected. Your game progress (leaves, stickers, badges, statistics) is stored only on your device, is not backed up to the cloud, and is lost if you uninstall the app or change device. Your scores and gardener name are still sent to the leaderboards. You can sign in with Google Play Games later from Settings → Account; your progress is then stored with your Unity Gaming Services player and carried across devices.
  • Gardener name: the nickname you choose in the game. It is shown publicly to other players on the leaderboards next to your scores.
  • Scores and leaderboards (via Unity Gaming Services Leaderboards): your game scores, weekly/monthly/all-time rankings and archived past rankings, together with the run statistics (pieces placed, lines cleared, duration) used to validate scores on our servers (Unity Cloud Code).
  • Analytics data (via Firebase Analytics): app interactions such as sessions, level results and purchases, device model, OS version, country (coarse, IP-based), and a resettable app instance identifier. Used to understand and improve the game.
  • Advertising data (via Unity LevelPlay and its ad network partners, including Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network): advertising identifier (AAID), IP address, device information and ad interaction events. Used to serve and measure ads, and — only with your consent where required — to personalize them.
  • Crash data (via Firebase Crashlytics): crash logs and device state, used to fix stability problems.
  • Purchases: in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive only an anonymized purchase confirmation — never your payment details.

Consent and your choices

  • Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), a consent dialog is shown before personalized ads are served; you can decline and receive non-personalized ads instead.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • You can remove all ads permanently via the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase.
  • Signing in with Google Play Games is optional: you can play as a guest and sign in later from Settings → Account. You can also sign out of your account at any time from Settings → Account. Local progress stays on the device until you delete the account or uninstall the app.

Data sharing

Account, leaderboard, analytics, crash and advertising data are processed by our service providers — Google (Play Games Services, Firebase), Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Leaderboards, Cloud Code; Unity LevelPlay) and the ad networks listed above — under their own privacy policies. Data is transferred over encrypted connections (TLS). We share data with no other third parties, except where the law requires it.

Data retention and deletion

Account and leaderboard data are kept while your account exists. Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months.

Deleting your account (signed in with Google Play Games): in the game open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. This immediately deletes your Unity Gaming Services player, your leaderboard entries and the game progress on the device. Alternatively, follow the steps on our account deletion page to request deletion by e-mail; we complete such requests within 30 days.

Guest players: the in-app delete option is only shown to players signed in with Google Play Games. As a guest, you can request deletion of your server-side data (anonymous Unity Gaming Services player, gardener name and leaderboard entries) by e-mail to zenleafgames@gmail.com with the subject "Block Garden account deletion" and your gardener name; see the account deletion page. Your progress on the device is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchase records are held by Google Play under its own terms; anonymized analytics that cannot be linked to you may be retained for the period above.

Children

Block Garden is not directed at children under 13. The app declares a target audience of 13+ on Google Play and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game evolves; the current version is always available at this address, with the effective date above.
