Support

Block Garden: Zen Puzzle · Developer: Zen Leaf Games · Google Play (Android) and App Store (iOS) · Last updated: 2 October 2026

Contact

E-mail zenleafgames@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and your gardener name (the name shown on the leaderboard). We usually reply within a few days.

My purchase did not arrive

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Remove Ads and the Starter Pack are restored on any device signed in to the same Apple or Google account. If leaves you bought are still missing after a restart, e-mail us with the receipt or order number from Apple or Google.

Refunds

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), so refunds are handled by them: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or your order history in Google Play.

Keeping my progress on a new device

Sign in from Settings — Game Center on iOS, Google Play Games on Android — and your leaves, stickers, badges and best score are saved to the cloud and restored when you sign in on another device. Guest progress is stored on the device and on an anonymous cloud profile; it is lost if you uninstall the app without signing in.

Ads

Remove Ads in the Shop permanently turns off banner and full-screen ads; rewarded videos stay available if you want the bonus. You can change personalized-ad and privacy choices at any time in Settings.

Deleting my account or data

Go to Settings > Account and tap Delete account (or Delete my data for guest players). Details: account deletion.

Privacy

See the privacy policy.

Destek

Sorularınız için zenleafgames@gmail.com adresine yazın. Cihaz modelinizi, uygulama sürümünü (Ayarlar ekranının altında) ve bahçıvan adınızı eklerseniz daha hızlı yardımcı olabiliriz.

  • Satın alımım gelmedi: Mağaza > Satın Alımları Geri Yükle. Sorun sürerse Apple ya da Google sipariş numarasıyla bize yazın.
  • İade: Ödemeleri Apple ve Google işler; iade talepleri reportaproblem.apple.com ya da Google Play sipariş geçmişinden yapılır.
  • İlerlemeyi yeni cihaza taşıma: Ayarlar'dan Game Center (iOS) ya da Google Play Games (Android) ile giriş yapın.
  • Hesap ve veri silme: Ayarlar > Hesap; ayrıntı için hesap silme sayfası.
